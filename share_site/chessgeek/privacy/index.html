We collect as little as the app needs: the photos you scan never leave your phone, and we never collect your location or contacts.

What we handle and why

On your phone only

Photos and screenshots you scan are read on your phone and never uploaded. The games downloaded for puzzles, the puzzles found in them, your puzzle progress, skill statistics, opening repertoire and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Puzzles → delete, removes them.

Loading your games from Lichess or Chess.com

When you connect an account by username, the app asks Lichess (lichess.org) or Chess.com for that player's public games. Your phone sends the username to them directly, along with your IP address as any internet request does; we don't receive it. Their own privacy policies apply. ChessGeek is not affiliated with Lichess or Chess.com.

Advertising

ChessGeek is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google receives information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google appears when you first open the app. If you agree, ads may be personalised; if you don't, Google shows limited or non-personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller of this data: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and for storing information on your device (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3)); our legitimate interest in funding a free app for limited ads.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used, the app counts a few actions (app opened, a scan read or failed, a puzzle solved or failed, a game analyzed, an opening studied, a post or comment made, a position shared) and sends only daily totals per action, platform and app version, for example “Android, version 1.0.1: 412 puzzles solved”. No account, device ID, advertising ID, location, position or text is included, and our server does not store the address a request came from. This only happens if you allowed measurement in the consent message (where one is shown); you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

Legal basis: your consent.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, the error details are saved on your phone. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Send report”. A report contains the error message and technical trace, the app version, the phone's operating system version and, if you're signed in to the community, your account ID.

Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the report.

Community account (optional)

If you create an account we process:

  • Email address and password, to sign you in and send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password). Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Username, profile picture and the date you joined, shown to other signed-in users.
  • Posts and comments (positions, solutions and text), shown to other signed-in users.
  • Follows, post alerts and blocks. Follows are visible to other users; blocks and alerts are private to you.
  • Reports you send, and reports others send about your content, including a copy of the reported content so moderators can review it.
  • Failed sign-in attempts by username (the username and the network address used), kept for one hour to stop password guessing.

Legal basis: providing the community you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interest in keeping it safe and moderating it (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

  • Account data, posts and comments: until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports and the copies of reported content: as long as needed to handle the report, then up to 12 months as a moderation record.
  • Crash reports: up to 12 months.
  • Failed sign-in attempts: one hour.
  • Usage totals: kept, as they contain no personal data.

Deleting your account

In the app: open the Community tab, tap the profile icon at the top right, then the menu (⋮) → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, profile, profile picture, posts, comments, follows, blocks and post alerts.

Without the app: email [support@chessgeek.com] from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do so within 30 days and confirm.

Reports about content you posted may be kept as described above. Puzzles and games on your phone are not affected; uninstall the app to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time (Settings → Privacy) without affecting what happened before. Write to [support@chessgeek.com]; we reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Latvia that is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija), www.dvi.gov.lv; you may also complain where you live.

Children

ChessGeek is meant for people aged 13 and over. The community requires an account and is not for children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each user change their own data, and other users can never see your email address. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

We'll update this page when the app changes what it handles, and change the date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
