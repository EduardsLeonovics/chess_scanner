ChessHive

The chess app that learns from your own games.

  • Scan a board from a photo or screenshot and analyze it with Stockfish, right on your phone.
  • Train on puzzles found in your own Lichess and Chess.com games.
  • See your skills and openings, and share puzzles with other players.
